VERDICT
- Flood evacuation: a flood pool that captures inflow above a per-dam release target and evacuates at that target
  (law FA) is a real, dam-specific effect at flood-control dams, but small. Over the per-dam bucket at all 166
  flood-control dams: +0.0073 [+0.0034, +0.0124], 124/37, controls 0.0000, DiD +0.0084 [+0.0045, +0.0115]. Over the
  rule curve: +0.0047 [+0.0019, +0.0088]. At the 117 on-reach DOR > 0.5 gauges it adds nothing at the median (+0.0006
  over the bucket; 0.0000 over the rule curve). It cuts the 30-120 d band error at flood-control on-reach DOR > 0.5
  dams by about a third of the excess over controls, but that improvement sits in a minority of gauges.
- Withdrawal at irrigation dams: NOT supported. A May-Sep withdrawal does nothing (0.0000 over the bucket, 13/23), it
  does not beat a plain per-gauge runoff scalar, and the scalar does not gain more at irrigation dams than at their
  controls. The data say irrigation dams store water in Oct-Apr and release MORE than the no-dam flow in Jul-Aug, the
  opposite of a summer diversion at the gauge.
- Combined per-dam law set (bucket + rule curve everywhere, plus the flood pool at flood-control dams): +0.0494
  [+0.0301, +0.0972] at the 117 target gauges (same median as the rule curve alone), +0.0250 [+0.0170, +0.0329] at all
  458 smoke dam gauges, and +0.010 on the POPULATION median offline (+0.005 after the engine discount).
- +0.03 on the population median is NOT reachable with dams plus runoff rescaling. Best offline estimate, per-gauge
  scalar at every gauge plus the full dam law set: +0.014 to +0.017; with the engine discount on the dam part, about
  +0.008 to +0.010. Per-HUC2 rescaling alone gives +0.0026; a global 0.9475 gives +0.0047.

2. FLOOD-EVACUATION LAWS (laws.c, v6.py, run_laws.py; laws_by_gauge.csv, summary.txt, lawset.txt, fig4)
Laws. Each sits on top of the per-dam bucket T0, with T0 refitted jointly. Per dam there is one extra state, the flood
pool F, starting empty.
- Capture: c_t = min(phi * max(I_t - Qc, 0), Fmax - F_t). Pass-through: p_t = I_t - c_t.
- FA, capacity evacuation (standard operating policy): e_t = min(F_t, max(Qc - p_t, 0)). Release is held at the target
  Qc until the pool is empty. Parameters: Qc = kc * Ibar, phi, Fmax = z * Ibar.
- FB, linear evacuation: e_t = F_t / (Te + 1), only while p_t < Qc, with an unbounded pool.
- FC: e_t = min(F_t / (Te + 1), max(Qc - p_t, 0)).
- Update: F_{t+1} = F_t + c_t - e_t, and the bucket receives x_t = p_t + e_t (minus the rule-curve flux in FA4/FC4).
Fit and score:
- Training NSE, grid (7 kc x 2 phi x 5 z/Te x 2 T0) then Nelder-Mead from the best 3 cells.
- Mass is conserved exactly and x_t >= 0 by construction (c <= I, e <= F), so the discharge floor is never used.
- Capture uses same-day inflow here. In the engine it uses the previous hourly step.

3. WITHDRAWAL LAW (irrigation n=40, water supply n=61; summary.txt, extras.txt, scalar_over_bucket.txt)
Law: W*_t = w * Ibar * s(doy), with s scaled to an annual mean of 1. It is taken from what the dam holds today,
W_t = min(W*_t, S_t + I_t), and removed on top of the bucket. The water leaves the system and is reported.
Shapes tested:
- W1: fixed smooth May-Sep window.
- W2: the dam's training-year inflow climatology inside Apr-Oct.
- W3: a window with per-dam centre and width.
Nulls, fitted on training years: a per-gauge scalar (Kg), a per-HUC2 scalar (Kh; fitted on the 916 smoke gauges,
kpool.json, k 0.88-1.00) and a global scalar (Kc 0.9475). Each was run alone and under a refitted bucket (L2K*).

5. RECOMMENDATION
Implement:
(a) Keep the per-dam T0, the existing dam row.
(b) Add the flood pool FA at gauged flood-control dams.
    - Per-dam parameters: kc (release target Qc = kc * Ibar), phi in [0, 1], z (Fmax = z * Ibar). Ibar is the
      detached training-mean model inflow at the dam.
    - State: F per dam, in m3.
    - Engine form, at step t+1: from step-t state take I_t (the dam's step-t inflow, upstream Q_t + q'_t) and F_t, and
      compute c = min(phi (I_t - Qc)+, (Fmax - F_t)/dt), p = I_t - c, e = min(F_t/dt, (Qc - p)+).
    - Solve with the dam row's q'_{t+1} replaced by q'_{t+1} - c + e. A and the CSR pattern are unchanged, and the
      O(nnz) backward is untouched; c and e are piecewise-linear RHS fluxes, like the rule curve.
    - After the solve: F_{t+1} = F_t + dt (c - e).
    - Mass is exact and the effective inflow is >= 0 by construction, so the floor is never needed.
    - F must be carried across test chunks. Initialise it at 0 (an empty flood pool) with a >= 30 d warmup in training
      windows. The release_eval.rs test table must carry kc, phi, z and Ibar per dam.
    - Expected gain over the bucket at flood-control dams: about +0.007 offline [+0.003, +0.012], roughly +0.004 in
      the engine. On the population median it adds about +0.001 to +0.003.
(c) The rule curve, if the extra 4 coefficients per dam are acceptable: +0.0036 on the population median offline
    over the bucket, about +0.002 in the engine.
Do not implement: FB/FC (no better than FA; FC loses at controls 47/87), any seasonal withdrawal law, or a learned
dam loss beyond an optional per-gauge k <= 1.
Expected population effect of the full recommended set: +0.005 (engine-discounted) to +0.010 (offline); with a global
or per-gauge runoff scalar, +0.008 to +0.017.
+0.03 is not reachable from dams plus runoff rescaling. The remaining room is at undammed gauges near the median, in
the routing head and in Q'.

CAVEATS
- The offline daily bucket sits on the routed gauge series, so the dam reach is double-counted. At off-reach dams the
  pool acts on the whole gauge series.
- Only the temporal hold-out is tested. Per-dam parameters apply only to gauged dams; ungauged dams were not tested.
- Training-year predictions do not exist for the population, so the HUC2 and global scalars were fitted on the 916
  smoke gauges' training years, and the per-gauge scalar is imputed for 1,449 gauges. The global scalar beat the
  per-HUC2 set on the population (+0.0047 vs +0.0026): HUC2 fits from the dam-heavy smoke sample do not transfer.
  A population training-year eval would settle this.
- Medians of paired gains are not additive (for example, FA4-L2 is not FA-L2 plus L4-L2).
- One seed of the no-dam model.
