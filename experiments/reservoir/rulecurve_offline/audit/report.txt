1. LEAKAGE: none found.
Code reading:
- Ibar = I[train].mean() (rc.py:74). The climatology uses train days only (rc.py:70).
- The training mask is train & finite(O) (rc.py:65). The fit simulation stops at n_fit = last train day + 1 (rc.py:64).
- The closed-form LS (rc.py:263-269) and the Nelder-Mead objective (rc.py:132-140) both see observations only through that mask.
- Test years are simulated only at scoring (rc.py:121-129, 334-337), continuing the state from inputs alone.

Empirical check (leak_test.py): at 6 random gauges I replaced the test-year observations with lognormal noise, tripled the test-year inflow and added 50. I then refit L1 and L4 with rc.py's own code. All fitted parameters and Ibar came back bit-identical.

Windows:
- The time axis runs 1981-10-02 to 2010-09-29, not 10-01 to 09-30 as the harness docstring says. The effect is harmless: spin-up is 364 d.
- Train is 4748 d. Test is 5478 d and starts at index 5112 = n_fit.

The inflow is the routed no-dam head, which was trained on 1981-10-01 to 1995-09-30 (/home/tbindas/projects/ddrs/.ddrs/runs/2026-09-12T23-39-03Z-train-and-test/config.yaml lines 68-69). The test years are therefore out-of-sample for the inflow model too. Observations are never an input.

Latent issue, inert here: rc.py:61 fills NaN inflow with np.nanmean over the whole series, test years included. The harness has the same pattern (expected_release_fit.py:86, ceiling.py:57). The predictions have 0 NaNs in 916 x 10590, so it has no effect. Fix it before reusing the code on inflow with gaps.

2. RECURSION: exact.
- Flux sign: x = I - r with r = Ibar * (c @ [sin w, cos w, sin 2w, cos 2w]) and w = 2 pi doy/365.25 (rc.py:68, 110, 127), as the law states.
- Floor: bucket.c:16 floors q at 0, and bucket.c:20 keeps S = avail - q, so a floored day keeps its deficit in storage and S may go negative. Mass is conserved; mean flux is zero, so beta is unchanged.
- Initial state: S = T0 * x_0 (bucket.c:10), set 364 d before training starts.
- C vs Python (check_recursion.py): I compared my pure-Python mass-balance loop, the shipped libbucket.so and a fresh gcc build of bucket.c.
  - Gauges: 10, including three with 70-71% floored test days and two controls.
  - Modes: feedback floor, no floor and engine clamp.
  - Result: max abs difference 0.0 with identical floor flags. My closed-form IIR (lfilter) equals the loop exactly.
- Re-scoring their fitted L0, L1 and L4 parameters with my simulator and metrics reproduces the test NSE at all 916 gauges to <= 4.4e-16. Ibar matches to 6e-14.

3. MASK AND L0: correct.
- metrics() masks test & finite(obs) & finite(sim) (rc.py:143-144). Simulations are always finite (no NaN inflow, T >= 0.05), so every law is scored on the same days.
- L0 is the routed prediction itself (rc.py:124-125). It matches the harness nse_pass_test to 0.0 at all 916 gauges.
- Minor inconsistency inherited from the harness: L1 is fitted with an unfloored T (rc.py:219) and scored with T >= 0.05 (rc.py:117). It moves test NSE slightly at 115/234 gauges; the median is +0.0267 either way.

4. CONTROLS: same procedure, but not comparable in baseline skill.
- run_fits.py:51-52 fits every smoke gauge with rc.fit_all. rc.py has no role-dependent branch, and bounds and grids are identical.
- Pairing is 1:1: control_for is unique and covers all 458 dams.
- The confound: the matched controls sit at median L0 NSE 0.726 against 0.337 at the dams, so they have little room to gain.
  - Among all 458 controls, L4 helps poorly modelled ones: +0.045 at L0 <= 0 (n=13), +0.008 at L0 0-0.3, +0.038 at L0 0.3-0.5, +0.002 at L0 > 0.7.
  - L1 gains about 0 at every L0 > 0 (-0.002 to 0.000); only the 13 controls with L0 <= 0 gain from it (+0.039).
  - Controls matched to each dam on baseline NSE (5 nearest of 458) gain +0.008 to +0.017 with L4, not +0.0045. With L1 and L2 they gain -0.001 to +0.003.
  - So part of the rule curve's gain corrects any seasonal model error, whatever its cause. The bucket part (L1/L2) is the dam-specific part.
