2. LAWS AND RECURSION
The bucket is the harness recursion (avail = S + x; q = avail/(T+1); S = avail - q) in C (bucket.c). Q is floored at 0 and the deficit stays in storage, so S may go negative (the dam is below its rule curve).

Fitting:
- Closed-form least squares for the flux coefficients on the unfloored bucket.
- Grid over T0 (40 log points, 0.05-365 d), times phi for L3b, times a and b for L5.
- Nelder-Mead refinement on the floored training NSE from the best 3 cells, within bounds.

Laws:
- L0: pass-through.
- L1: seasonal-T bucket.
- L2: plain bucket.
- L3: flatten, f in [0, 1.5]. C is the 31-d circular moving average of the training-year doy mean of the model inflow I.
- L3b: flatten plus phase phi in [-90, 90] d.
- L4: harmonic rule curve, coefficients in [-3, 3].
- L4r: rule curve only, with T0 held at the 0.05 d floor.
- L5: L4 plus seasonal T.

Variants (run_variants.py):
- ec: fitted and scored with an engine-like clamp that keeps S = T Q, so a floored day forgets its deficit.
- lin: no floor.
- pen: training floored-day share held <= 1%.
- asec: L4's parameters re-scored under the clamp, with no refit.

8. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE ENGINE
a. Where the flux enters. The affine law S = T Q + S0(doy) only changes the right-hand side of the dam row: subtract S0(t+1) - S0(t) = dt*r. A (the system matrix) and the CSR pattern are unchanged, and the O(nnz) backward is untouched.
b. No state at window starts. S0 is a function of doy, so the closed-form start S = T Q + S0 still holds. rho windows and test chunks need no state, unlike any long-memory law.
c. Needs per dam:
   - A scalar Ibar: the training-mean model inflow at the dam, e.g. the mean of upstream Q', detached, as a precomputed per-dam table or head input.
   - sin 2w and cos 2w, from the existing seasonal_phase by double angle.
   - It does NOT need a Q' climatology table, because L3 failed.
d. The clamp:
   - The existing post-solve clamp_min on Q (no feedback) reproduces the gain (L4ec +0.0507), but only when the parameters are trained under that clamp (L4asec +0.0276).
   - The clamp creates mass: at DOR>0.5 on-reach the median is 0.04% of test inflow, the 75th percentile 2.0% and the 90th percentile 21%.
   - Recommendation: keep the clamp and add a loss penalty on clamped dam-row days. L4pen shows <= 1% floored days costs nothing.
   - A storage-deficit state with feedback is not required.
e. Head outputs: log T0 from NID features, plus 4 coefficients (bounded, for example 1.5*tanh). Because features cannot predict the coefficients, emit them from a per-dam free parameter table (an embedding, initialised at 0) trained on the gauge loss.
   - This is legitimate: gauge observations are the target, not an input.
   - Expect it to help only at dams constrained by a training gauge.
   - Report ungauged-dam performance separately. For those dams the ceiling is the T0-only bucket, about +0.021 at DOR>0.5.
f. The trap noted in the earlier review still applies. The test-phase table in release_eval.rs must carry the 4 coefficients and Ibar per dam.

CAVEATS
- Offline, daily step. The bucket sits on the routed gauge series, which already routes the dam reach (engine T ~ K_reach, not 0).
- Test years follow training years (temporal hold-out only).
- Controls are matched by HUC2 and area, not by irrigation or withdrawal intensity. Part of the dam-associated seasonal signal could be withdrawals co-located with dams.
- The medians of per-gauge gains are skewed: the paired L4-minus-L1 median is +0.0095, not +0.023.
